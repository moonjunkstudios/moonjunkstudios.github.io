BASE JUMP: FREEFALL

The game is drawn as moving pictures, so its menus can't be read by a screen reader yet. It opens on the course menu: use the up and down arrow keys and Enter to choose a course (Widowmaker Canyon is first; the others unlock in order). In flight: Space jumps and opens the canopy, W and S dive and pull up, A and D bank, Q and E barrel-roll, hold Shift for tricks. Escape or P pauses; R restarts. The Controls button lists every key.
