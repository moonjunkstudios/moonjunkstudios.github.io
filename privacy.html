Privacy

Last updated 12 November 2026. Short version: we only count visits and gameplay if you say yes, we never see who you are, and we don't show ads.

Analytics: not chosen yet.

What we use

This site uses Google Analytics 4, run by Google, to count visits and see how the game is played. It loads only after you click Allow. If you click No thanks, or your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, nothing is loaded and no analytics cookies are set.

What is collected (only if you allow it)

  • Visits: pages viewed, how you arrived (for example a search, a link or a campaign tag in the link), and roughly where and on what kind of device (country and city level, browser, screen size). Google does not store your IP address in Analytics.
  • Clicks to other sites: when you click our Ko-fi link or one of our social profiles.
  • Gameplay summaries in BASE JUMP: FREEFALL: the game loaded, a course was started, how the run ended (landed, completed, crashed or restarted), the score, the medal, how long the run lasted, and whether you finished the tutorial.
  • Returning visits: a random ID in the _ga cookies lets Analytics tell a new visitor from a returning one. It is not linked to your name, email or any account.

What is never collected

  • Your name, email or any account. The game has no accounts.
  • Your keystrokes or controls, or anything you type.
  • Your saved game. Your progress stays in your own browser.
  • Advertising data. Ads features, ad personalisation and Google Signals are switched off.

Cookies and storage

  • _ga and _ga_G8VVEFEW93: Google Analytics, set only after you allow it, kept for up to 13 months.
  • mj_analytics (your browser's local storage): remembers your yes or no, so we don't ask on every page. We ask again after 13 months.
  • The game saves your progress and settings in your browser's storage. That stays on your device and is never sent to us.

Where the game runs

This notice covers moonjunkstudios.com only. If you play BASE JUMP: FREEFALL on a games portal such as CrazyGames, our analytics is switched off there, and that portal's own privacy policy applies.

Keeping and deleting data

Analytics data is kept for 14 months, then deleted by Google. To stop collection, click Turn analytics off above: it removes the Analytics cookies from this site. You can also clear this site's cookies in your browser, or use Google's opt-out add-on. Google's own use of the data is described in Google's privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or requests about your data: message us through our Ko-fi page. Because we don't know who you are, we usually can't find your data in Analytics, but turning analytics off stops any more being collected.
